# Additional supervised encoders under 800,000,000 bytes

## Which encoder is worth testing next?

### Takeaway
Recommend **Ettin-encoder-68m**, as a separately predeclared supervised-reader experiment, not as a runtime-ready model or a claimed stronger diagram reader. If a published browser graph is required before starting, Ettin-32m is the cheaper exploratory alternative, but its published graph is a masked-language model, not the necessary diagram reader. A small supervised encoder is the more directly aligned next test than a tiny generative LLM: the task is already specified as calibrated action, value, span, role and cut prediction; this is a task-fit inference, not a measurement of LLM quality or speed. [Current reader](../../modernbert.md); [Ettin release](https://github.com/jhu-clsp/ettin-encoder-vs-decoder); [Ettin paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2507.11412).

### Cited findings
- ModernBERT's frozen evaluation was **16 right, 0 wrong, 1,625 held and 104 unread out of 1,745**, with no extras. It trained for one epoch; shipped DistilBERT had eight and different optimization. This negative product/pipeline result does not establish intrinsic encoder superiority or that more training would repair it. Keep all existing artifacts and gates. [Report](../../modernbert.md).
- Ettin-68m is a 19-layer, 512-hidden, 8-head bidirectional ModernBERT-family masked-LM, with local attention 128 and global attention every third layer; its config declares FP32. Its native weights are 273,868,198 bytes, native weight plus config/tokenizer inventory 276,023,908 bytes. The official tree has no ONNX graph. A targeted `onnx-community/ettin` search found published 17m and 32m encoder graphs, not a 68m one; this is not proof no other conversion exists. **Browser complete bundle NOT VERIFIED**. [68m official card](https://huggingface.co/jhu-clsp/ettin-encoder-68m/blob/ac19ae4bc51093b31c475665ac872a936d056cc2/README.md); [config](https://huggingface.co/jhu-clsp/ettin-encoder-68m/blob/ac19ae4bc51093b31c475665ac872a936d056cc2/config.json); [immutable file tree](https://huggingface.co/api/models/jhu-clsp/ettin-encoder-68m/tree/ac19ae4bc51093b31c475665ac872a936d056cc2?recursive=true&expand=true); [community search](https://huggingface.co/api/models?search=onnx-community%2Fettin&limit=30).
- The Ettin publishers compare matched encoder/decoder families and report a base-sized GLUE mean 88.9 versus ModernBERT-base 88.4; this is their benchmark claim, **not a 68m diagram result**, and aggregate GLUE does not establish accurate roles or calibrated execution. [Publisher card](https://huggingface.co/jhu-clsp/ettin-encoder-68m/blob/ac19ae4bc51093b31c475665ac872a936d056cc2/README.md); [paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2507.11412).
- The practical supervised test needs trained joint action/value and token span/role/cut heads, exported again with tokenizer parity and calibrated abstention, against the same annotated messages and fixed comparable risk/coverage protocol. A sentence embedding, retrieval or NLI/QA checkpoint is not a diagram parser; do not replace role parsing with cosine similarity. [Current interface](../../../README.md); [MiniLM embedding card](https://huggingface.co/sentence-transformers/all-MiniLM-L6-v2/blob/1110a243fdf4706b3f48f1d95db1a4f5529b4d41/README.md).

### Inferences
- Ettin-68m balances a materially smaller base than ModernBERT with the existing ModernBERT-family tokenization/export experience. Its size is not proof of faster end-to-end execution or greater task accuracy. The successful next experiment would require a separately authorized, preregistered protocol and comparisons at equal execution precision, not a change to the frozen ModernBERT gate.
- Smaller weights can reduce download and storage, but depth, sequence length, attention implementation, activations, quantization and device determine time and memory. Do not derive measured latency or runtime memory from file size.

### Gaps
- No diagram-reader fine-tune, export, parity, calibration, benchmark, latency, memory, native CPU, WebGPU or browser WASM test was run for any proposed candidate. There is no evidence that Ettin-68m beats this repository's readers; original weights are not a ready-to-load `makeReader` artifact.

## What was actually published and how much does it cost?

### Takeaway
Four community ONNX exports have **model-file inventories** below the strict limit for their listed quantized variant; NeoBERT's FP32 model inventory exceeds it. None is yet a verified complete download of a supervised browser reader. Exact pinned paths, publisher-reported byte sizes, source hashes and alternate FP32 inventories are in [encoders-artifacts.json](encoders-artifacts.json).

URLs for each individual file are the exact repo/revision/path resolve URLs defined there. No weight files were downloaded. The sums count one chosen graph and its declared matching data shard, not all optional quantizations; auxiliary tokenizer files are conservatively counted even when a loader might not request them. [Manifest](encoders-artifacts.json).
- Filename `model_quantized.onnx` denotes a published quantized-storage variant, not proof of INT8 arithmetic, runtime support, graph parity, exact operator selection or precision of every tensor. Similarly `model.onnx` is listed as the full-precision alternative, not a measured memory footprint. The separate full-precision native source dtype is only stated when the source config declares it. [Manifest](encoders-artifacts.json); [prior ModernBERT arithmetic failure and repair](../../modernbert.md).
- Raw task-agnostic `microsoft/MiniLM-L12-H384-uncased` is a plausible WordPiece supervised alternative, with native `pytorch_model.bin` 133,483,028 B, but its official tree has no ONNX, and this research did not verify a browser export. It is not interchangeable with all-MiniLM-L6's sentence-embedding weights. ### Inferences
- Ettin-32m is the lowest-friction published modern-family artifact to investigate, not automatically the best model. DeBERTa offers an architectural contrast at larger tokenizer and graph cost; NeoBERT is a larger comparator, not a small-model latency bet. Sentence-trained MiniLM is best treated as a distinct cheap control.

### Gaps
- The **complete required model bundle** includes the final supervised graph, all its external tensors/shards, and required tokenizer/config files; its sum must be strictly below 800,000,000 B. JS/WASM runtime assets and dependencies are counted separately, outside that model ceiling. Exact required model bytes and operator support are unknown until an actual export and frozen loader exist. The inventories above are strictly below 800,000,000 B but are **not proof of complete-bundle eligibility**; the final measured model sum must be strictly less, not equal. HF tree absence of other `.onnx_data` files does not prove graph references were inspected. Native weights themselves are not a browser bundle.
- Browser CPU WASM: unverified for these exact graphs/versions; WebGPU: separately unverified, including operator placement; native CPU: separately unverified. Existing project ModernBERT has specific WASM evidence but that does not transfer to Ettin or another export. [Existing result](../../modernbert.md).

## What can break integration?

### Takeaway
Train and export the actual joint outputs, then test the exact tokenizer, graph and runtime together; an ONNX listing is not a browser-parity receipt.

### Cited findings
- `makeReader` requires a `reader` config, exactly two special tokens from empty input, per-word first-piece alignment, `seq_logits`, `token_logits` and a cut head; BPE uses a prefix-space policy and declared padding ID, with explicit truncation. The modern training code preserves UTF-16 offsets and the same wordwise policy. DeBERTa's nonzero pad ID and SentencePiece tokenization and Ettin's own BPE/special IDs must be checked rather than assumed compatible. [Reader](../../../docs/js/neural.js); [training tokenization](../../../training/neural/modernbert_tokenization.py); [DeBERTa tokenizer config](https://huggingface.co/onnx-community/deberta-v3-small/blob/cd592b4177c55afa3068f2316f0096a3b687f996/tokenizer_config.json); [Ettin tokenizer config](https://huggingface.co/onnx-community/ettin-encoder-32m-ONNX/blob/37b0e13e959679e9bd8a5cf4b7b49eda17cd5042/tokenizer_config.json).
- The existing ModernBERT q8 dynamic-activation graph failed browser parity; its repaired graph used weight-only uint8 storage with FP32 arithmetic and Basic graph optimization. Thus ONNX, quantized size, or a publisher's Transformers.js example alone does not demonstrate semantic parity. [Report](../../modernbert.md).
- Prior broader research and the neighboring `vsa-semantic-space` browser prototype were inspected read-only; the latter's masked-token comparison is not a diagram task and its old raw MiniLM export was pending. These sources were not treated as current artifact inventories. Prior encoder notes: `browserLLM/docs/browser_encoder_notes/contextual_encoders.md:3,136–156`; neighbor browser README: `browserLLM/vsa-semantic-space/browser/README.md:1–3,16–22,33–60`.

### Inferences
- Risk points include SentencePiece word boundaries and padding, BPE leading-space behavior and partial words, custom NeoBERT model/export code, rotary/sliding-attention ops, quantized matrix operators and backend-specific fusion. Validate request and cut boundaries, Unicode character offsets, roles, batching, truncation and confidence on Python, native CPU and each browser backend separately.

### Gaps
- Published export cards do not establish the new supervised graph's logits contract, full required browser transfer, task parity, or per-backend behavior. No weight graph was opened to inspect its operators or external-data references; no large weight download or runtime test was performed.
